# Agriculture News Images

To complete the news section, add these images to the `/public/assets/news/` directory:

1. `rice-harvest.jpg` - Image of rice harvesting
2. `coconut-farming.jpg` - Image of coconut farming
3. `organic-farming.jpg` - Image of organic farming practices
4. `climate-resilient.jpg` - Image of climate-resilient crops
5. `fisheries.jpg` - Image of aquaculture/fisheries
6. `agri-tech.jpg` - Image of agricultural technology

These images will be displayed in the news items in the left sidebar.
